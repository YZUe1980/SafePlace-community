# SafePlace 🌍

**Global intelligence. Local safety.**  
**Status: Concept & research — not yet released.**

SafePlace is a planned international Android app to make real-world risk information easier to understand. Its vision combines a location-focused safety dashboard, a global map, careful AI research, forecasts based on real information, emergency contacts and offline guidance.

> **Important:** SafePlace is not available for download, does not currently issue live warnings, and must never be used as a substitute for official emergency services or warning channels.

## What we want to build

- **One tap to assess:** a manual "Check now" action; no background monitoring in the initial release.
- **A global map:** switchable map styles, hazard categories and relevant developments, with an option to view all.
- **Seven safety dimensions:** weather & natural hazards, geopolitics, radiation & environment, infrastructure, public health, public safety, space & technology.
- **Explainable AI scores:** seven proposed 0–100 rings. Scores would be reasoned from actual sources, not generated from fixed arbitrary weights or fabricated when data is missing.
- **Careful AI forecasts:** scenarios based on real events across 24 hours, 7, 30 and 90 days, and one year. Facts, forecasts and AI hypotheses must be clearly distinguished.
- **Emergency & help:** a dedicated area for location-relevant verified emergency phone numbers and practical guidance.
- **Offline resilience:** downloadable maps, country packs, emergency phrases and previously saved reports with visible timestamps.
- **International by design:** German, English and Turkish are candidate initial UI languages.
- **Privacy first:** usable without mandatory accounts or personal risk profiles.

## How this public repository is used

This is the **public community and communication space**, not the source code repository. It hosts the high-level public roadmap, design concepts, project news, contribution guidance and feedback.

The development repository remains private. Internal research, proprietary implementation, API keys, user/location data and unpublished security work must not be copied into this repository.
