# Public brand and media usage

**Working name:** SafePlace (name and trademark clearance pending)  
**Public descriptor:** Global intelligence. Local safety.  
**Release state:** In development / Concept preview.

Media assets in `media/` are conceptual marketing visuals, **not functioning Android screenshots**. Any ring/phone elements should carry no fabricated risk readings or live status claims.

Approved claims:
- An envisioned global, AI-assisted situational awareness app.
- Planned seven hazard categories and explainable 0–100 safety indicators.
- Planned offline emergency assistance and international support.
- Planned ability to review original data sources and uncertainties.

Avoid:
- "Download now", "Available on Google Play", store badges or app download QR codes.
- Claims of guaranteed safety, real-time automated alerts in the first version, or official certification.
- Fake incident reports or numbers presented as genuine current safety assessments.
- Disclosure of internal architecture, provider contracts or unpublished strategies.

**Rights:** Artwork and text remain reserved pending an explicit licensing decision. Do not republish third-party logos, emergency agency marks or copyrighted photographs without permission.
