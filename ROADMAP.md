# Public Roadmap

**This is a proposed sequence, not a release schedule or a promise of shipping dates.**

## 1. Vision & source validation — underway
- Clarify safety information and the separation of fact, forecast and AI hypothesis.
- Evaluate official international hazard data coverage, licensing and freshness.
- Design a risk-score interface with seven 0–100 rings, separate uncertainty and clear missing-data states.
- Check naming and trademark viability.

## 2. Product and safety design — planned
- Define manual assessment workflow and transparent source explanations.
- Review warnings, offline behavior and emergency-number quality.
- Design accessible map views and compact reports.
- Validate score comprehension and avoid false precision.

## 3. Technical research — planned
- Compare Android mapping and offline-data options.
- Evaluate replaceable AI providers, query cost and privacy protections.
- Define tests for inconsistent, incomplete and stale external information.

## 4. Development and verification — not started
- Implement only after explicit project approval.
- Test usability, geographic relevance, translated emergency guidance, security and reliability.
- Obtain appropriate legal and licensing review before any public distribution.

## 5. Public release — not announced
- Publish only after readiness, safety and store-policy reviews.
- Keep critical official warning information and emergency assistance accessible without a paywall.

> **No fictional hazard simulation** is planned as a user feature. Exploratory AI reasoning must remain anchored to real source information.
