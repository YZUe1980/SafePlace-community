# Community Code of Conduct

SafePlace welcomes constructive participation from people with different professional backgrounds and regions.

- Treat others respectfully; challenge claims and reasoning without attacking people.
- No harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing or publication of private information.
- Do not knowingly spread fake emergency alerts, manipulated evidence or dangerous medical advice.
- Present uncertain information as uncertain and cite original sources where possible.
- Respect moderator requests and keep discussion relevant to the project.

Report conduct concerns through a private GitHub contact mechanism if available; avoid posting private details publicly. Maintainers may hide or remove harmful content and restrict participation. This document is a project policy proposal, not a guarantee of constant moderation.
